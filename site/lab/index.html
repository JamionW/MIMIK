Mimik lab: on-device interpreter benchmark

Runs speech recognition and translation to English on this phone, with no server. Keep the screen on and the app in front while a test runs. Models download once per configuration (sizes below); use Wi-Fi.

1. Benchmark suite

Each configuration downloads its model, then transcribes and translates 8 recorded replies per language (56 clips), and matches each transcript against the language's known replies. Run all reloads the page between configurations, since a model's memory is not given back otherwise, and carries on by itself, also after a crash (marked with the step it reached). Finished and crashed configurations are skipped unless you tick redo. The WebGPU ones crashed on iOS 26 and only run from their own buttons.

2. Live microphone

Have someone say a line in the chosen language, or play one from a video. Hold the button while they speak and release when they stop. The page shows the known reply it matches, with its English, or "not sure"; the time runs from release to that answer. Try lines from the app's replies and lines that are not in it: the second kind should come back as "not sure".

3. Battery soak (10 minutes)

Unplug the phone and note the battery percentage. This transcribes clips back to back for 10 minutes, far more than real use, so it is the worst case. Enter the percentage before and after.
