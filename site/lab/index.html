Mimik lab: on-device interpreter benchmark

Runs speech recognition and translation to English on this phone, with no server. Keep the screen on and the app in front while a test runs. Models download once per configuration (sizes below); use Wi-Fi.

1. Benchmark suite

Each configuration downloads its model, then transcribes and translates 8 recorded replies per language (56 clips). If the tab reloads mid-run, that configuration crashed: it is marked with the step it reached, and Run all remaining moves on to the next. Finished and crashed configurations are skipped unless you tick redo.

2. Live microphone

Have someone say a line in the chosen language, or play one from a video. Hold the button while they speak and release when they stop. The time shown runs from release to the English appearing.

3. Battery soak (10 minutes)

Unplug the phone and note the battery percentage. This translates clips back to back for 10 minutes, far more than real use, so it is the worst case. Enter the percentage before and after.
